# Nuhaya & Raed

Two iOS apps for Muslim families.

**Nuhaya** — a habit and reward chart for children. Parents set habits, children
earn stars, stars become coins, coins are saved toward something real.

**Raed** — prayer times, Qibla and dhikr, calculated on your device and working
fully offline.
