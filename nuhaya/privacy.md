# Nuhaya — Privacy Policy

*Last updated 7 September 2026*

**Nuhaya does not collect your data. There is no server, no account, and nothing is
sent to us.** Everything the app stores stays on your device.

This policy is short because there is little to describe. That is deliberate.

## What the app stores, and where

All of it is on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage:

- **For each child:** a first name, an **age band** — under 6, 6 to 8, or 9 to 11 —
  and which of the bundled illustrations they picked as an avatar.
- Their habits, the stars they have been awarded, their coins, what they are saving
  toward, and any promises they have made.
- The rewards a parent has offered, and requests for them.
- Notification preferences.
- A **passcode**, stored in the iOS Keychain as a salted hash. The passcode itself is
  never stored, and never leaves the device.

### What it deliberately does not store

- **No date of birth.** An age band is all the app needs, so a birthday is not asked
  for.
- **No photographs of children.** Avatars are illustrations that ship with the app.
- **No email address, no phone number, no postal address, no location.**
- **No account.** A child never signs in to anything.

## What is not in the app

- **No advertising.** No ad networks, no ad identifiers.
- **No third-party analytics.** No Firebase, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no
  equivalent. Apple's Kids Category prohibits them and we would not use them anyway.
- **No tracking**, across apps, websites, or anywhere else.
- **No social features.** A child cannot message anyone, and there is no way for
  anyone to contact a child through this app.

## What Apple sees

If you have agreed in your iOS settings to share analytics with developers, Apple
gives us **aggregate, anonymous** figures — how many devices installed the app, how
many opened it, crash reports. We cannot see individuals in that, and it is Apple's
measurement, not ours. You can turn it off in **Settings → Privacy & Security →
Analytics & Improvements**.

## Children's privacy

Nuhaya is intended for use by children under the supervision of a parent, and is
built to comply with COPPA and the UK and EU rules on children's data.

The app collects **no personal information from a child**. A child's first name and
age band are entered by their parent, stay on the parent's device, and are never
transmitted. Because nothing is collected or shared, there is nothing to request
deletion of from us — but see below.

## Deleting everything

- **Delete everything** in the parent's settings clears all family records
  immediately.
- **Deleting the app** removes all of it, including the passcode.

Neither requires contacting us, because we hold nothing to delete.

## Changes

If the app ever gains a feature that changes any of this — syncing between a family's
own devices through their iCloud account, for instance — this policy will be updated
before that feature ships, and the date at the top will change.
